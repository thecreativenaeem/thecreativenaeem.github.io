# StrongerHer: a focused launch plan

Prepared for Naeem and Coach Hebah · 12 September 2026

**Recommendation: send this campaign's traffic directly to StrongerHer.** Use the Coach Hebah homepage as the broader brand introduction, with a prominent route into StrongerHer. For a flyer promoting a specific six-week program, adding a homepage step creates another decision before a visitor can assess that program. This is a recommendation based on the described campaign, not a measured conversion result.

## The small-scale funnel

```text
Existing clients share the flyer in WhatsApp communities
                         |
          Direct link to /strongerher
                         |
            Understand offer and see proof
                         |
      Before enrollment opens: join the waitlist
                         |
      Receive confirmation + useful program details
                         |
      Enrollment opens: clear invitation to enroll
                         |
        Payment / personal enrollment assistance
                         |
           Confirmation and onboarding

Instagram is a parallel trust-building route:
Flyer → Instagram profile → StrongerHer link → same funnel
```

Do not require everyone to visit Instagram first. Some referred visitors will already trust the recommendation and want the practical details immediately. Instagram should reassure those who choose to investigate.

## What V2 changes now

- Larger primary CTAs: 64–68px minimum height, with 17–18px labels in the main conversion areas.
- Larger reading text: generally 17–18px on desktop and 16–17px on mobile; supporting labels have their own smaller scale.
- A desktop header waitlist CTA and a text-first mobile hero.
- The existing women-only audience label in the hero.
- Testimonials immediately after the hero, before the detailed method.
- Community screenshots after the coaching explanation, preserving proof without placing a second long gallery before the offer details.
- Stronger section headings, larger testimonial controls, larger FAQ questions, and more legible member names and captions.
- A clearer final signup panel with a secondary WhatsApp contact route.

All original media, copy, complete testimonials, and FAQ answers are preserved. The client-approved wordings still need a separate commercial-content pass: V2 does not invent dates, pricing, availability, claims, or a sales video.

## What the page needs before enrollment opens

1. **A clear offer summary.** Confirm cohort dates, schedule and time zone, class duration, weekly commitment, equipment, recording-access period, price and currency, and what is included. Explain optional versus required sessions. Resolve the current three-session versus 2–4-workout discrepancy.
2. **The correct primary action for the campaign stage.** A waitlist is useful before enrollment. When visitors can buy, move the primary action to a real enrollment route. Keep WhatsApp as the option for questions. Do not launch a purchase campaign with only a vague waitlist destination.
3. **A dependable next step after every action.** A confirmed signup should tell her what will arrive and when. A successful purchase should provide enrollment confirmation, receipt, onboarding instructions, and support contact information. Do not equate a button click with a successful lead or payment.
4. **Trust tied to the offer.** Retain real member stories. Ask permission for any new identifiable screenshots or video testimonials. Make coach qualifications and relevant experience easy to verify. Do not add unsupported guarantees or assume one member's weight-loss result is typical.
5. **Decision clarity.** Explain beginner suitability, privacy, optional fasting, recording access, and available modifications close to the relevant section. Include the approved cancellation/refund terms before payment.
6. **A short, specific sales video when ready.** A proposed 60–120-second video can cover who the program is for, a brief class example, what six weeks include, common concerns, and the next step. Use captions and a deliberate play action. A video supports the offer; it does not replace missing schedule, price, or enrollment information. No video asset was supplied, so V2 contains no pretend player.
7. **A good mobile sharing experience.** Confirm the flyer URL, page title, social-preview image, phone loading performance, email delivery, and behavior inside Instagram and WhatsApp browsers. The portable review HTML embeds full original media; publish the optimized asset-based implementation instead.

## Instagram and WhatsApp: one coherent message

Use the same program name, visual identity, offer promise, and campaign stage on the flyer, profile, and landing page. Set the Instagram bio's primary campaign destination to StrongerHer. Suggested pinned-post roles are: an introduction to the program, real member proof, and how participation works. Suggested highlights are the program, results, questions, and the coach.

Give previous clients one approved flyer and a short forwarding message with one campaign link. The forwarding text should state who it is for, what the six-week program involves, and how to learn more. Let the client approve the text. Avoid asking advocates to explain conflicting logistics individually.

## A lean follow-up sequence

Start with the existing MailerLite account rather than introducing several new tools. MailerLite documents form-completion and group-joining triggers; confirm the actual form and subscription settings before connecting the workflow. [MailerLite automation triggers](https://www.mailerlite.com/help/how-to-set-up-automation-triggers)

| Moment | Message purpose | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed signup | Welcome; clarify confirmation status and what happens next | View program details |
| About a day later, if enrollment is still pending | Explain a sample week and the main participation concerns | Read details or ask a question |
| Later in the prelaunch period | Share one relevant member story and answer a common objection | Review suitability |
| Actual enrollment opening | State confirmed dates, price, inclusions, and deadline | Enroll |
| Before the real closing date | Give a factual reminder to people who have not enrolled | Enroll or ask a final question |
| Confirmed purchase | Receipt, welcome, equipment and access instructions | Complete onboarding |

These are proposed message roles, not configured automations. Align timing to the actual launch; do not keep new subscribers in a long prelaunch sequence after enrollment has opened. Suppress enrollment reminders for confirmed purchasers. Keep operational onboarding separate from promotional follow-up, and respect unsubscribe preferences. For this first cohort, a small manually maintained enrollment list may be enough; automate payment-to-enrollment updates when volume justifies it.

## Measure the useful steps

Use consistent campaign tags for the direct flyer and Instagram-bio links, then verify that analytics receives them. Google's documentation explains the source, medium, and campaign parameters. [Google Analytics campaign URLs](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952)

Suggested direct-flyer URL:

`https://coachhebah.com/strongerher?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=strongerher_launch`

Suggested Instagram-bio URL:

`https://coachhebah.com/strongerher?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=strongerher_launch`

Track landing visits, confirmed leads, WhatsApp contact clicks, confirmed paid enrollments, and revenue per cohort. Distinguish a contact click from an actual conversation. Track form attempts separately from confirmed leads, and checkout attempts separately from purchases. Forwarded links can identify the originating campaign, not reliably every individual who forwarded them. No analytics tracking has been deployed as part of V2.

For the first cohort, review absolute counts and the questions people ask. With a small audience, use those observations to prioritize improvements before attempting fine-grained A/B tests or assuming conversion-rate changes are statistically meaningful.

This is a proposed sequence, not a delivery commitment. Confirm the actual launch date before assigning calendar deadlines.

## What to defer

A membership app, large on-demand library, elaborate CRM, paid advertising, extensive automations, and an agency-style ongoing growth engagement can be separate later decisions. The initial trial should prove that one offer can attract inquiries, capture leads, enroll participants, and remain easy to maintain.

Studio Era is useful as a directional reference for prominent benefit statements, repeated actions, and visible proof. Its subscription/trial offer and product scope differ from this six-week cohort. Keep StrongerHer's own voice, colors, and women-only positioning. [Studio Era](https://www.studioera.co/)

## Editing together without endless back-and-forth

**For this launch: stay in Lovable and test the editing workflow with Hebah before committing to it.** Keep the project under the agreed client ownership and give Naeem appropriate collaborator access. Use comments attached to the preview for feedback. Lovable supports project-sharing roles. [Lovable project sharing](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/share-project)

The current Lovable documentation describes a preview toolbar with direct inline text editing. Selecting an element and requesting a styling change is a separate, AI-assisted workflow. Therefore, promise direct wording edits, but demonstrate font-size and spacing changes in the actual project rather than describing Lovable as a completely unrestricted manual visual editor. [Lovable preview toolbar](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/preview-toolbar)

Acceptance exercise: Hebah changes a heading, corrects a paragraph, adjusts one text-size setting, previews mobile, and restores the previous version with Naeem observing. Document the successful steps. If deterministic size controls are essential, scope a small authenticated settings interface with predefined typography choices and editable content fields; that interface is additional implementation, not already part of this HTML preview.

For a future platform review, Framer offers on-page editing for content such as text and images. Design and text-style work may still involve its editor. Trial it with her real edits before deciding to migrate. It is not automatically a reason to change platforms two weeks before launch. ## The payment question

Her question most likely concerns **how she pays Naeem for the trial**. Naeem's accepted methods, invoicing currency, and terms have not been provided, so no payment provider or commercial terms are represented as already available.

Answer with the actual method you can receive, agreed invoice currency, and the milestone arrangement you are willing to offer. Separately confirm how participants pay for StrongerHer. Check the client's merchant location, currency, existing payment account, fees, settlement requirements, and supported countries before recommending a provider. Do not assume a payment platform is available just because the website can link to it.

## A bounded paid trial

Propose one StrongerHer page implemented from the approved V2, one working signup/enrollment route, a small agreed follow-up sequence, essential measurement, and a client-editing handover. Specify revision rounds, responsibilities for assets and copy, access requirements, payment terms, and the review/publishing process in the agreement. Treat broader brand strategy, social management, ads, a membership platform, and ongoing optimization as separately scoped work.

The trial's success criteria should be concrete: approved mobile presentation, reliable signup, a clear path to payment, correct onboarding, and the client's ability to make the agreed routine edits herself.
