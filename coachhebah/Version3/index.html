01 / TRAIN

01TRAIN

The Intentionally Stronger 2–2–1 Training System

2 Strength. 2 HIIT. 1 Pilates/Mobility. One complete training week — in just 3 sessions.

Build real muscle. Improve your conditioning. Strengthen your core. Transform your body with balanced, athletic training — all from home.

You don't need to train like a man to train seriously. We use dumbbells at home and progressive overload to build muscle and power.

Strength and HIIT are intelligently combined to build strength and conditioning without turning five training components into five separate workout days.

Pilates and mobility develop your core strength, provide another full-body resistance-training stimulus, and keep your joints flexible and strong.

02FAST

The Sunnah Weight Loss Method

Fast twice a week the way the Prophet ﷺ taught us — and learn how to use those fasts strategically for fat loss.

No complicated fasting schedules. No eating windows to obsess over. A simple, powerful rhythm that helps create the calorie deficit that drives fat loss — without restricting yourself every day.

Forget keto. Carnivore. Cutting out carbs. And whatever diet trend comes next.

Learn how your metabolism, hunger and fat loss actually work — so you're no longer jumping from one set of diet rules to another.

ThenNow

I’m Coach Hebah — fitness instructor, mother of five, and creator of StrongerHer and the Intentionally Stronger training system.

My own transformation started long before StrongerHer. I lost 80 pounds (36 kg) and went on to build a career in fitness, teaching thousands of classes and staying active through five pregnancies.

But in my 40s, my body changed again. I gained weight I couldn't seem to shift using the same strategies that had always worked for me.

That experience ultimately changed the way I approach fat loss. By combining athletic training, Sunnah fasting, real food and a better understanding of my metabolism, I lost the final 10 pounds — without living on a diet.

At 48, I’m leaner, fitter and stronger than I was at 38.

Now I teach Muslim women the system I wish I'd had years ago.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Contact
Do I need to be fit before joining?

No.

Beginners are welcome, and all exercises can be modified to your current fitness level.

Whether you're just getting started or returning to exercise after a long break, you'll be able to participate and progress at your own pace.

I already work out. Will I still benefit?

Yes.

Many women in my programs already exercise regularly but struggle with consistency, progression, nutrition, or seeing the results they want.

You'll benefit from the coaching, structure, accountability, nutrition education, and community support built into the program.

What if I have knee pain, back pain, injuries, or medical conditions?

Many exercises can be modified to accommodate common aches, pains, and limitations.

However, if you have a medical condition, recent surgery, pregnancy, or any situation that may affect your ability to exercise safely, you should obtain clearance from your healthcare provider before participating.

Do I need equipment?

For best results, I recommend having a set of light, medium, and heavy dumbbells.

Ideally:
• One light pair
• One medium pair
• One heavy pair

If that isn't possible, start with whatever you have available and we will help you make it work.

How much time will this take each week?

Most women attend 2–4 live workouts per week and one nutrition session.

You can participate at the level that fits your schedule, and all sessions are recorded if you need additional flexibility.

Do I have to fast?

No.

The nutrition system includes optional Sunnah fasting principles, but modifications are available based on your individual circumstances and goals.

You can still benefit from the program even if fasting is not appropriate for you.

Do I need to follow a special diet?

No.

The program is built around real food and practical habits that fit into everyday life.

You do not need to purchase special foods, meal replacements, or follow an extreme diet.

What if I don't want to lose weight?

That's perfectly fine.

While many women join to lose fat, the program is also designed to help you build strength, improve fitness, increase energy, improve your health, and develop sustainable habits that support long-term wellbeing.

What if I miss a class?

Life happens.

All workout sessions and nutrition sessions are recorded, so you can catch up when it suits your schedule.

What if I can't attend live?

You can still participate.

All workouts and nutrition sessions are recorded, so you'll continue to have access to the coaching and content even if you occasionally miss a live session.

Are recordings included?

Yes.

Workout recordings and nutrition session recordings will be available throughout the program.

Do participants sign a privacy agreement?

Yes.

All participants will be required to agree to the program's privacy guidelines before joining.

This includes respecting the privacy of Coach Hebah and fellow participants and not sharing access links, recordings, screenshots, or program materials outside the program.

Will I appear in the recordings?

Workout and nutrition sessions will be recorded for participants who cannot attend live.

Participant video will not be featured in the recordings. However, participant audio may occasionally be captured if questions are asked during class or discussion.

For this reason, please avoid sharing any personal information that you would not want other program members to hear.

Recordings are provided exclusively for StrongerHer participants and may not be shared, distributed, downloaded, reposted, or used outside the program.

All participants agree to respect the privacy of Coach Hebah and fellow members by not sharing recordings, screenshots, class links, or program materials outside the community.

Do I have to turn my camera on?

No.

Many women choose to keep their cameras off.

However, if you turn your camera on, Coach Hebah can provide real-time coaching, form corrections, and more personalized guidance during class.
